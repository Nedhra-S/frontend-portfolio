The Daily Campus Times

Tuesday, 3 November 2026

Students Launch Campus-Wide Web Design Challenge

More than five hundred students have signed up for a week-long challenge to design and build websites for local community groups, with mentors from the front end development course guiding each team.

New Library Hours Announced

The central library will remain open until midnight during exam weeks. Study rooms can now be reserved online up to three days in advance.

Chess Club Wins Inter-College Trophy

After five tense rounds, the campus chess club took home the championship trophy, with the final decided in a thrilling tie-break.

Green Campus Drive Plants 2,000 Saplings

Volunteers from several departments joined the weekend drive, planting saplings around the hostels and sports ground.

Hackathon Registrations Open

Teams of up to four can register for the annual hackathon. Themes include education, healthcare and sustainable living.

Cycling Club Plans Weekend Ride

Riders of all levels are invited to a 40 km scenic route starting at the main gate on Sunday morning. Helmets are mandatory.

Cafeteria Introduces Healthy Menu

The main cafeteria has added millet-based dishes, fresh salads and fruit counters following a student survey on healthier food options.
